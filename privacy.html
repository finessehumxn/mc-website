Privacy Policy

Effective July 28, 2026. This policy describes how Millennials Creatives LLC handles information on millennialscreatives.com.

What we collect

Information you give us directly: your name, email address, and message when you contact us, request enrollment, or join a waitlist, and order information when you purchase. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

To deliver what you bought, respond to your messages, provide support, and send updates you have asked for. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, to anyone, ever.

Who we share with

Only the service providers that make the site work: Stripe for payment processing and our website hosting provider. Each handles your data under its own privacy policy. We share information beyond that only if the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses only the technical cookies needed for basic functions such as checkout. If we add analytics in the future, this policy will be updated first.

Your choices

Email us anytime to ask what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and every marketing email we ever send will include an unsubscribe link. This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.
