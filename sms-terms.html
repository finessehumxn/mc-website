SMS Terms and Conditions

About this program

Millennials Creatives LLC, a technology and creative services firm based in Phoenix, Arizona, operates a text messaging program for customers, clients, and people who ask to hear from us.

Depending on how you opted in, messages may include replies to questions you sent us, appointment reminders and project updates for active engagements, and occasional news about our services, courses, and software products.

How you consent

We only text people who have given express written consent. You provide that consent when you submit your mobile number through a form on our website, check a box requesting text updates, provide your number and agree to texts as part of a service agreement, or send us a text message first.

Consent is not a condition of purchase. You can buy anything we sell and work with us on any project without agreeing to receive text messages.

Only the person who owns or is the authorized user of a mobile number may opt that number in.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. The number of messages you receive depends on how you engage with us and on the services you have active. Conversational replies are sent only in response to messages you send us.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Millennials Creatives LLC does not charge for text messages, but your mobile carrier may charge you for sending or receiving them depending on your plan. Contact your carrier for details about your rates.

How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any message from us at any time. You will receive one confirmation message, and then we will stop sending texts to that number.

If you want to start again later, opt in the same way you did the first time, or reply START to the same number.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message from us and we will reply with support information and contact details.

You can also reach us any time by phone at 602-800-0660 or by email at contact@millennialscreatives.com.

Supported carriers and delivery

Our program supports major and regional carriers in the United States. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to effective transmission by your mobile carrier and to network availability, so we cannot guarantee that every message arrives.

Your privacy

We treat your mobile number as private information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared only with service providers who help us operate the messaging program, such as our texting platform, and those providers are prohibited from using it for any other purpose.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party except as required to deliver the messages you asked for.

Our full Privacy Policy explains how we handle the rest of your information.
